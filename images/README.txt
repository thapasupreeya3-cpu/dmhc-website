TO REPLACE WITH REAL PHOTOS
---------------------------
Just overwrite any file above with your own photo, keeping the same
file name. Landscape 3:2 shape, at least 1200 x 800 pixels, JPG.

IMPORTANT: because the current images are AI-generated, they are not
labelled as "our team" anywhere on the site. When you have a real
team photo, put it in as photo-team.jpg and ask Claude to update the
caption to say it's your team - authenticity matters to families
and to the NDIS Commission.

Always get written consent from anyone appearing in real photos
(your Participant Intake Form's photo consent covers participants).
